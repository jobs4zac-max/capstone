# Know Your Customer (KYC) & Customer Due Diligence Policy
**Document ID:** POL-CMP-KYC-004  
**Regulatory Baseline:** Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) / FinCEN CDD Rule / USA PATRIOT Act  
**Classification:** Customer & Operational Guidance  

---

## 1. Policy Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the standards and procedural mandates for obtaining, verifying, and updating Customer Information Files (CIF) throughout the lifecycle of customer relationships at Apex Financial.

---

## 2. Trigger Events for Mandatory KYC Refresh
Customer profiles undergo mandatory re-verification under any of the following occurrences:
1. **Periodic Review:**
   * High-Risk Risk Profile: Annual review (every 12 months).
   * Moderate-Risk Risk Profile: Triennial review (every 36 months).
   * Low-Risk Risk Profile: Quinquennial review (every 60 months).
2. **Event-Driven Reviews:**
   * Legal name change (marriage, divorce, court decree).
   * Change of residential address across state borders or international relocation.
   * Material change in financial activity (e.g., unexplained 300% surge in monthly transactional volume).
   * Power of Attorney (POA) designation or court-ordered conservatorship.
   * Dormant account reactivation after 24 consecutive months of inactivity.

## 4. Digital Submission & Document Processing Workflows
1. **Portal Upload:** Customers submit high-resolution color captures (JPEG, PNG, or PDF) via the secure Apex Banking Mobile App or Client Web Portal.
2. **Biometric Liveness Check:** Digital submissions for remote identity updates require a real-time facial biometric scan matched against the photo on the primary identity document.
3. **Review SLA:** Compliance and operations teams process submissions within two (2) standard banking days.

---

## 5. Non-Compliance and Account Restrictive Actions
Failure to satisfy periodic or event-driven KYC requests results in graduated operational restrictions:

* **Day 1 to 30 Post-Notice:** Initial notifications distributed via email, SMS, and portal banners. Account remains fully functional.
* **Day 31 to 45:** Second warning. Restrictions placed on initiating outbound external wire transfers and credit limit increases.
* **Day 46 to 60:** Account status altered to **Restricted-Debit-Freeze**. Inbound deposits accepted; outbound debits, card purchases, and ATM withdrawals blocked.
* **Day 61+:** Formal relationship wind-down initiated. Account balances transferred to an escrow ledger; official bank draft mailed to the last confirmed address pursuant to state unclaimed property and abandonment regulations.
