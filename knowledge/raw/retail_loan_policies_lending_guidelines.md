## 2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) Calculation Model
The Debt-to-Income ratio evaluates an applicant's contractual monthly obligations against verified gross monthly income:

$$\text{DTI} = \frac{\sum \text{Contractual Monthly Debt Service}}{\text{Gross Verified Monthly Income}} \times 100$$

* **Included in Debt Obligations:** Housing expense (rent or primary mortgage principal, interest, taxes, and insurance), auto installments, student loans, minimum revolving credit card payments, child support/alimony.
* **Excluded:** Variable utilities (electric, cellular, broadband), life/auto insurance premiums, discretionary living costs.

## 3. Mandatory Income Verification Guidelines
1. **W-2 Wage Earners:**
   * Most recent consecutive pay stubs reflecting minimum 30 days of year-to-date earnings.
   * Last one to two years of IRS Form W-2.
2. **Self-Employed / 1099 Contractors:**
   * Minimum two (2) complete tax years of personal and business federal tax returns (IRS Form 1040 with Schedules C/E, Form 1120-S, or 1065).
   * Year-to-date unaudited Profit and Loss (P&L) statement.
3. **Non-Employment Income (Pensions, Disability, Annuities):**
   * Official award letters or benefit verification statements accompanied by proof of consistent direct deposits for the trailing three (3) months.

## 4. Hardship Modification, Forbearance, and Deferral Protocols
Borrowers experiencing severe verified disruptions (e.g., job involuntary termination, declared natural disaster, prolonged hospitalization) may request hardship accommodations:

* **Short-Term Payment Extension (Deferral):** Up to two (2) non-consecutive monthly payments deferred per 12-month rolling period. Interest continues to accrue; loan maturity date extends by the number of deferred cycles.
* **Rate-Reduction Restructure:** Qualified long-term distress cases may transition to an interest-rate step-down plan for a fixed duration of 6 to 18 months, reducing monthly cash-outflow constraints.
* **Prerequisites:** Account must have been maintained in good standing for at least six (6) consecutive cycles prior to the hardship onset date. Requests require supporting validation documentation submitted within 14 calendar days of application.
