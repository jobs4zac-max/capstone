# Apex Horizon High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA)
**Document ID:** PRD-SAV-2026-V3  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Audience:** Retail Banking Clients & Customer Support Specialists  
**Classification:** Public Information  

---

## 1. Product Overview
The **Apex Horizon High-Yield Savings Account** is engineered to help account holders grow liquid reserves at competitive market rates while maintaining seamless access for milestones, unexpected events, and planned major transitions.

### Core Value Propositions
* **Tiered APY:** Earn up to $4.85\%$ Annual Percentage Yield (APY) compounded daily and credited monthly.
* **Zero Maintenance Fees:** No monthly recurring account maintenance fees or minimum balance penalties.
* **Goal-Linked Vaults:** Partition a single account balance into up to ten sub-savings pockets (e.g., "Wedding", "Down Payment", "Parental Leave").

---

## 2. Interest Rate Tiers & Yield Structure
Interest is calculated on the daily collected ledger balance using the daily balance method. 

| Tier | Balance Band | Base APY | Relationship Bonus APY* | Total Maximum APY |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | $\$0.01 - \$9,999.99$ | $4.10\%$ | $+0.25\%$ | $4.35\%$ |
| **Tier 2** | $\$10,000.00 - \$49,999.99$ | $4.35\%$ | $+0.25\%$ | $4.60\%$ |
| **Tier 3** | $\$50,000.00 - \$249,999.99$ | $4.60\%$ | $+0.25\%$ | $4.85\%$ |
| **Tier 4** | $\$250,000.00+$ | $3.75\%$ | $+0.15\%$ | $3.90\%$ |

*\*Relationship Bonus applies to accounts maintaining a recurring monthly direct deposit of at least $\$1,000.00$ or an active Apex Premier Checking link.*

## 3. Account Eligibility & Documentation
To qualify for an Apex Horizon Savings Account, primary applicants must satisfy the following:
* **Age:** Minimum 18 years of age (or legal age of majority in jurisdiction).
* **Tax Identification:** Valid Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN).
* **Residency:** Valid domestic street address (P.O. boxes acceptable only as mailing addresses with physical residential proof on file).
* **Minimum Opening Deposit:** $\$25.00$ within thirty (30) days of approval.

---

## 4. Withdrawal Limits & Fund Availability
Although federal Regulation D transaction limit enforcement has been relaxed, Apex Financial institutes internal liquidity protocols to safeguard capital integrity:
* **Electronic Fund Transfers (ACH Out):** Up to $\$50,000$ per business day.
* **Instant Internal Transfers:** Unlimited between linked Apex checking and savings accounts.
* **Incoming Check Deposits (Mobile):** Standard clearance window of 1 to 2 business days; amounts exceeding $\$10,000$ subject to a 3-business-day hold.
* **Wire Transfers:** Domestic outgoing wires incur a $\$15.00$ processing fee; international outgoing wires incur a $\$35.00$ fee.

---

## 5. Life-Event Automated Milestone Features
Customers experiencing major life changes can activate targeted savings tools:
1. **Pacing Engine:** Auto-adjusts monthly transfer recommendations based on an inputted milestone date (e.g., child due date, wedding date).
2. **Surge Safeguard:** Allows temporary deposit pauses of up to 90 days without losing preferred bonus APY tiers during documented employment disruption or medical leaves.
