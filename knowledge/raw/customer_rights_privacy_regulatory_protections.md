## 1. Electronic Fund Transfers (Regulation E Protections)
Apex Financial ensures customer protection against unauthorized electronic account debits (ATM withdrawals, point-of-sale transactions, unauthorized ACH debits) under federal Regulation E.

### Consumer Liability Schedules
The extent of a consumer's legal liability for unauthorized electronic fund transfers is determined directly by the promptness of their reporting:

| Notification Window | Maximum Consumer Liability |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Prior to unauthorized use** (card/credential loss reported early) | $\$0.00$ |
| **Within 2 business days** of learning of access device compromise | Up to $\$50.00$ |
| **Between 3 and 60 calendar days** after transmission of bank statement | Up to $\$500.00$ |
| **After 60 calendar days** of transmission of bank statement | **Unlimited liability** (Full loss balance past 60 days) |

### Provisional Credit Guarantees
Upon notice of an unauthorized electronic transfer, Apex reviews the claim and issues provisional credit to the impacted account within **ten (10) business days** if the ongoing investigation requires additional time (up to 45 or 90 days for POS/foreign transactions).

## 2. Fair Credit Reporting & Error Resolution (FCRA & Reg Z)
Consumers have legal protections when obtaining loans or carrying revolving debt balances:
* **The Right to Know:** You are entitled to an annual free disclosure of your complete credit file from each nationwide credit reporting agency via `AnnualCreditReport.com`.
* **Adverse Action Disclosures:** If Apex denies an application for credit or decreases your credit line, we must provide an Adverse Action Notice detailing the primary credit score, score factors, and specific reasons for the decision within thirty (30) days.
* **Billing Error Rights:** Inquiries regarding contested credit card or line-of-credit billing statements must be transmitted within sixty (60) days of statement delivery. Apex acknowledges receipt within thirty (30) days and completes investigation within two complete billing cycles (max 90 days).
