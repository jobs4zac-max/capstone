## Life Event 1: Marriage and Legal Partnerships

### 1.1 Structural Account Integration Models
Couples have three common architectures for organizing marital cash flows:
* **Complete Consolidation:** All income streams route to a singular joint checking and savings ecosystem. Maximum transparency, requires absolute alignment on discretionary spending.
* **Proportional Equity (The "Three-Bucket" Model):** Each partner maintains an individual discretionary checking account. A shared joint account covers joint liabilities, funded in proportion to respective gross incomes:
  $$\text{Contribution Share}_A = \frac{\text{Income}_A}{\text{Income}_A + \text{Income}_B} \times \text{Joint Overhead}$$
* **Siloed Autonomous:** Separate finances with an itemized, divided distribution of household expenses.

### 1.2 Actionable Transition Checklist
- [ ] Update beneficiary designations on 401(k), IRA, HSA, and life insurance policies.
- [ ] Submit legal name and address changes via the Apex KYC Update Portal.
- [ ] Conduct joint estate planning: Last Will & Testament, Durable Financial Power of Attorney, Healthcare Proxy.
- [ ] Evaluate tax filing strategies: Married Filing Jointly (MFJ) vs. Married Filing Separately (MFS).

## Life Event 2: Welcoming a Child (Birth / Adoption)

### 2.1 Immediate Cash Flow & Liquidity Buffers
* **Healthcare Deductible Reserve:** Segregate maximum out-of-pocket (MOOP) health insurance costs in an accessible HYSA sub-vault at least 90 days prior to due date/adoption finalization.
* **Parental Leave Bridge:** Calculate net deficit during unpaid or partially paid leave (FMLA / short-term disability) and pre-fund an auxiliary checking buffer.

### 2.2 Long-Term Asset Allocation & Tax Strategy
* **529 Qualified Tuition Plans:** Establish a tax-advantaged 529 account. State income tax deductions apply in eligible states. Assets grow federal tax-free when applied to qualified higher education expenses.
* **Dependent Care Flexible Spending Account (DCFSA):** Maximize pre-tax payroll contributions (up to $\$5,000$ per household) for eligible nursery, daycare, and after-school programs.

## Life Event 3: Navigating Divorce or Marital Separation

### 3.1 Financial Asset Separation & Preservation Protocols
1. **Establish Independent Credit & Banking Footprint:** Immediately open sole-ownership checking and savings accounts at a financial institution distinct from joint accounts to prevent inadvertent asset freezes.
2. **Inventory Assets and Liabilities:** Document real estate equity, qualified retirement plans, taxable brokerage accounts, revolving debt, and secured notes.
3. **Credit Shielding:** Request joint credit card accounts be closed or downgraded to authorized-user removals to prevent unilateral debt accumulation.
4. **Qualified Domestic Relations Orders (QDRO):** Ensure retirement plan divisions (pensions, 401(k)s) are executed under explicit court-approved QDRO terms to prevent premature distribution penalties and tax obligations.

### 4.1 Retirement Asset Portability Protocols (401(k) Rollover Matrix)
When terminating employment, four distinct routes exist for company retirement plans:
* **Direct Custodian-to-Custodian Rollover to Traditional IRA:** Retains tax-deferred status, eliminates risk of mandatory 20% federal withholding, expands investment selection.
* **Rollover to New Employer Plan:** Consolidates balances; keeps options open for back-door Roth strategies without violating the IRS *pro-rata* rule.
* **Maintain Balance in Former Plan:** Permissible only if vested balance exceeds institutional plan thresholds (typically $\$5,000$).
* **Lump-Sum Liquidation:** **Not advised.** Triggers immediate ordinary income tax, mandatory withholding, and a 10% early withdrawal penalty if under age $59\frac{1}{2}$ (unless qualifying under the Rule of 55).
