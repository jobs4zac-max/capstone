1. PURPOSE AND PRINCIPLES
===============================================================================
The real "approved bank policies" do not exist for a capstone, so the team
authors a SYNTHETIC but realistic NovaBank knowledge base. It must be small
enough to build in days, rich enough to exercise every behaviour in the SRS,
and deterministic enough that tests can check answers against known facts.

P1  All content is fictional. Add a footer to every doc:
    "Synthetic content for the LEFN capstone. Not real banking policy."
P2  Every testable fact has a FACT ID (e.g. NB-POL-001.F2) anchored in the
    document with an HTML comment. Tests reference fact IDs, not free text.
P3  The KB is internally consistent EXCEPT for the designated fixtures (s.6).
P4  Separate authorship: the person who writes the KB SHOULD NOT write the
    golden test cases (avoids circular evaluation). Cross-review each other's work.
P5  Only status=approved AND audience=customer documents are retrievable for
    customer answers (SRS FR-5.2).
P6  Documents are short (<= ~700 words), use H2 headings that double as
    citation sections, and use short declarative sentences (easy to cite).
P7  The KB deliberately has GAPS (s.7) so the agent's refusal to invent can be
    tested.

AUTHORING RULES
  - One FACT per sentence; anchor comment on the line above the sentence.
  - Use the exact numbers in Section 5 (tests depend on them).
  - Each doc has at least: Overview + (Eligibility | Requirements | Procedure).
  - Mention "cannot be completed through chat" where a procedure needs a
    human/branch/app action; the agent must never imply it can do it.
  - No customer names, real bank names, real regulations or real URLs.

===============================================================================
4. CHUNKING AND INDEXING SPECIFICATION
===============================================================================
Splitter     : heading-aware. One chunk per H2 section; if a section exceeds
               600 tokens, split by paragraph with 60-token overlap.
               Minimum chunk size 40 tokens (merge tiny sections upward).
chunk_id     : <doc_id>#<section_slug>-<n>          e.g. NB-POL-001#eligibility-0
Embedded text: "<title> > <section>\n<chunk text>"  (contextual header improves
               retrieval; the header is NOT shown to the customer).
Embeddings   : text-embedding-3-small, batch size 64, cached on disk.
Index        : FAISS (faiss-cpu) built with FAISS.from_documents(...);
               persisted with save_local("data/faiss_index_lefn") and reloaded
               with load_local(..., allow_dangerous_deserialization=True) only
               from the team's own build directory.
Filtering    : metadata filter dict supports life_event (list-contains, or
               "ALL"), doc_type, country (list-contains or "ALL"), product.
               status=approved and audience=customer are ALWAYS applied in
               the retriever wrapper (not left to the caller/LLM).
Retrieval    : top_k=5 (default), optional MMR (fetch_k=20, lambda=0.6).
Score        : convert FAISS distance to relevance in [0,1]; store in
               PolicyChunk.score. Record the mapping in NB01.
Rebuild      : python -m lefn.rag.index --rebuild (lint must pass first).
Internal docs: audience=internal docs (e.g. NB-POL-071) go to a SEPARATE small
               index or a plain file read used only by the escalate node;
               they are never returned to the customer-answer retriever.

SEED FACTS (author each fact as one sentence; use these exact values)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NB-POL-001 Joint Account Policy
  F1 Any two adults (18 or older) may hold a joint everyday or savings account.
  F2 Adding a co-holder requires government ID for both holders and a signed Joint Account Form.
  F3 Joint account requests are processed within 5 business days of receiving complete documents.
  F4 Unless a different mandate is set, either holder may operate a joint account alone.
  F5 Joint accounts cannot be opened, changed or closed through chat.

NB-POL-002 Beneficiary Designation and Updates
  F1 Beneficiaries can be named on NovaBank savings accounts and term deposits.
  F2 A change requires a signed Beneficiary Change Form and valid government ID.
  F3 Beneficiary Change Forms are reviewed within 10 business days.
  F4 If a beneficiary is under 18, the guardian's name and ID details must be provided.
  F5 Beneficiary changes cannot be completed through chat; they are submitted by secure message or at a branch.

NB-POL-003 Personal Details and Name Change
  F1 A legal name change requires an official supporting document (for example a marriage certificate) and government ID in the new name.
  F2 Debit cards are reissued free of charge after a legal name change and arrive within 10 business days.
  F3 Name changes are submitted by secure message in the app or at a branch.

NB-POL-030 Financial Hardship Support
  F1 Customers who lose employment may request a hardship review.
  F2 Eligible customers can have the monthly everyday-account fee waived for up to 3 months.
  F3 Payment holidays on NovaBank loans are considered case by case by the Hardship Team and are not automatic.
  F4 Supporting documents are a termination letter or an unemployment-benefit statement.
  F5 Hardship reviews are handled by a human representative.

NB-POL-070 KYC and Documentation Requirements
  F1 Accepted identification is a passport or a national ID card.
  F2 Proof of address must be dated within the last 3 months (utility bill, rental contract or official letter).
  F3 Documents not in the local language require a certified translation.
  F4 Marriage and birth certificates are accepted as supporting documents but do not replace government ID.

NB-POL-071 Escalation and Human Handoff (INTERNAL)
  F1 Requests for personalized investment, tax or legal advice must be referred to a human representative or licensed advisor.
  F2 Escalated requests receive a first response within 2 business days.
  F3 Escalation tickets must include a summary so customers do not have to repeat information.
  F4 Fraud reports are routed to the Fraud Response Team as urgent.

NB-POL-072 Customer Privacy and Data Protection
  F1 NovaBank does not disclose one customer's account or financial information to another customer, including family members.
  F2 Joint-account holders may access information about their joint account.
  F3 A spouse's individual accounts are accessible only to that spouse after authentication.
  F4 NovaBank staff and assistants never ask for or reveal PINs or passwords.

NB-POL-073 Fraud and Suspicious Activity
  F1 Suspected fraud must be reported immediately using the 24/7 fraud line listed in the NovaBank app, and cards can be blocked in the app.
  F2 Fraud cases are handled by the Fraud Response Team.
  F3 NovaBank never asks customers to move money to a "safe account".

NB-PRC-021 Salary Payment Update
  F1 To receive salary in a NovaBank account, the customer gives the employer that account's IBAN; NovaBank cannot change payments made by an employer.
  F2 Salary-linked benefits are recalculated after one full monthly cycle of qualifying credits.
  F3 Standing orders and direct debits stay on the original account until the customer updates them in the app.
  F4 Changing employer does not require closing an existing account.

NB-PRC-024 Moving Standing Orders and Direct Debits
  F1 Standing orders can be edited or recreated in the app under Payments.
  F2 New direct debit mandates must be authorised by the payee; NovaBank cannot transfer existing mandates.
  F3 Changes take up to 10 business days to become effective.

NB-PRC-050 Address Change (same country)
  F1 Address changes are submitted by secure message in the app or at a branch.
  F2 Proof of address dated within the last 3 months is required.
  F3 Cards and correspondence are updated within 5 business days.

NB-PRC-051 Relocating Abroad: Account Continuity
  F1 NovaBank supports customers in Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands and Ireland.
  F2 Customers moving between supported countries keep their accounts and must update their address.
  F3 Customers moving to a country not on the supported list must notify NovaBank at least 30 days before the move.
  F4 For moves to unsupported countries, the Relocation Desk (a human team) decides case by case whether accounts continue, are converted or are closed.
  F5 NovaJunior and NovaFamily Saver require the account holder to reside in a supported country.

NB-FEE-052 Transfers, Fees and Limits
  F1 SEPA transfers are free of charge.
  F2 Non-SEPA international transfers cost a flat fee of EUR 15.
  F3 The online transfer limit is EUR 10,000 per day; higher amounts require an advisor or a branch.
  F4 The chat assistant cannot initiate or execute transfers.

NB-PRC-060 Pension Deposit Setup
  F1 The customer gives the account IBAN to the pension provider; NovaBank does not contact pension providers.
  F2 Proof of retirement (a pension award letter) is required to switch to the NovaSenior account.
  F3 NovaBank does not calculate or confirm pension amounts.

NB-PRD-010 NovaFamily Saver
  F1 The variable interest rate is 2.1% per year.
  F2 There is no minimum balance and no monthly fee.
  F3 It may be held individually or jointly.
  F4 The account holder must reside in a supported country.

NB-PRD-011 NovaJunior Savings Account
  F1 It is for children under 18 and must be opened by a parent or legal guardian.
  F2 Required documents are the child's birth certificate and the guardian's government ID.
  F3 The variable interest rate is 2.5% per year with no fees.
  F4 Guardian authorisation is required for withdrawals while the child is under 16.
  F5 At age 18 the account converts to a standard NovaBank savings account.
  F6 The rate does not vary by the child's age. (No age-tiered rates are documented.)

NB-PRD-012 NovaEdu Fixed Saver
  F1 It is a fixed 5-year deposit with a fixed rate of 3.0% per year.
  F2 The minimum deposit is EUR 500.
  F3 Early withdrawal incurs a penalty of 1% of the amount withdrawn.

NB-PRD-020 Everyday Account and Fee Schedule
  F1 The monthly account fee is EUR 4.50.
  F2 The fee is waived when qualifying credits are at least EUR 1,000 per month or the average balance is at least EUR 2,500.
  F3 An overdraft is available on request, subject to credit assessment.
  F4 Card replacement costs EUR 10, except after a legal name change or fraud.

NB-PRD-022 Salary-Linked Fee Waiver Terms
  F1 Qualifying credits are salary or pension payments of at least EUR 1,000 per month paid into the account.
  F2 If qualifying credits are missing for 3 consecutive months, the EUR 4.50 fee resumes after 30 days' notice.

NB-PRD-031 Overdraft Terms
  F1 Overdraft limits are reviewed annually and may change after a change in income.
  F2 Overdraft decisions are made by credit assessment, not automatically.
  F3 The arranged overdraft rate is 14.9% variable APR.

NB-PRD-040 Personal Loan Summary and Eligibility
  F1 Loan amounts range from EUR 1,000 to EUR 30,000 with terms of 12 to 84 months.
  F2 Applicants must be 18 or older, reside in a supported country and have at least 6 months of income history.
  F3 Every loan is subject to credit assessment and approval is not guaranteed.
  F4 Loan decisions are made by NovaBank underwriters; chat cannot approve loans.

NB-PRD-041 Mortgage Overview
  F1 Mortgage applications are handled by a Financial Services Advisor.
  F2 Joint applications require income documents for both applicants.
  F3 NovaBank does not provide mortgage pre-approval through chat.

NB-PRD-060 NovaSenior Everyday Account
  F1 It is available to customers aged 60 or over, or with proof of retirement.
  F2 There is no monthly account fee.
  F3 Existing customers switch by secure message or at a branch.

NB-PRD-061 Savings and Investment Products Overview
  F1 NovaBank offers term deposits (2.8% per year for 12 months) and NovaInvest Funds.
  F2 The value of NovaInvest Funds can go down as well as up and capital is not guaranteed.
  F3 Personal investment recommendations are provided only by licensed Financial Services Advisors.
  F4 Opening an investment account requires an appointment with an advisor.

NB-FAQ-080 Life Events at NovaBank: Overview (topic map used for CONFIRM_OFFER)
  F1 Marriage: review joint account options, beneficiaries, and name-change documentation.
  F2 New child: review the NovaJunior and NovaEdu savings options and beneficiaries.
  F3 Job change: review salary payment details, salary-linked fee waiver and standing orders.
  F4 Job loss: review hardship support, overdraft terms and the fee waiver status.
  F5 Relocation: review address change, relocating abroad, and standing orders.
  F6 Retirement: review the NovaSenior account, pension deposit setup, beneficiaries and investment overview.
  F7 Escalated requests receive a first response from a representative within 2 business days.

10. INGESTION PIPELINE AND KB LINT
===============================================================================
Pipeline: read files -> parse front matter -> LINT -> chunk -> embed -> FAISS
          build -> persist -> smoke retrieval test (1 query per life event).

LINT RULES (all must pass; python -m lefn.rag.lint):
 L1  Front matter present and valid against Section 3 schema.
 L2  doc_id unique and equals the file name.
 L3  status approved => approved_by and approved_date present.
 L4  effective_date not in the future.
 L5  At least one H2 heading; every H2 has body text.
 L6  Every seed fact ID from Section 5 exists as an anchor in its document
     and the anchored sentence is non-empty (fact registry test).
 L7  No fact anchor IDs outside the registry (no orphan facts).
 L8  No PII patterns (IBAN, email, phone) in KB text (except fixture FX-INJECT-2).
 L9  Footer "Synthetic content..." present.
 L10 No document (other than fixtures) contradicts another on the same fact
     attribute (rate, fee, age, deadline): automated check compares numeric
     facts in the registry against document text.
 L11 Life event tags are valid enum values or ALL.
 L12 Draft/superseded documents are NOT in data/kb (they live only in
     data/kb_fixtures).
Smoke test: for each life event, query "<event> banking considerations" and
assert at least one approved chunk with matching life_event tag is returned in
top 5, and that no superseded/draft/internal chunk is returned.
